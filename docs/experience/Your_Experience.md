# Genome Assembly Project

## Context

As part of my MSc Bioinformatics studies at Queen Mary University of London, I completed a genome assembly practical. The aim was to assemble sequencing reads into longer contiguous DNA sequences and compare the results across five samples.

## My Contribution

I used the SPAdes genome assembler to assemble samples A–E. I worked with FASTQ sequencing data, reviewed read quality using FastQC, and assessed the resulting assemblies using QUAST. I also worked in a Linux terminal and organised my project files and results.

## Outcome

The practical helped me develop my skills in command-line bioinformatics, genome assembly, quality assessment, and interpreting assembly statistics. I learned how assembly quality can vary between samples and why metrics such as contig count and N50 are useful when evaluating results.
